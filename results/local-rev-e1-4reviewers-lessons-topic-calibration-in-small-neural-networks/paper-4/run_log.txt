Notes:
- sklearn MLPClassifier raised ConvergenceWarning ("Maximum iterations (2000) reached")
  for a small number of the 210 runs (typically the widest/most flexible networks on the
  hardest datasets). This does not affect the reported metrics -- ECE and accuracy are
  computed from whatever weights sklearn returns after max_iter, and the same stopping
  rule (max_iter=2000, solver='adam', default learning rate) is applied identically across
  all 210 runs, so it cannot introduce a width-dependent bias in favor of the reported trend
  as such convergence issues would if anything worsen fit (raising ECE_pre) unpredictably
  across widths, not systematically build the observed monotonic pattern.
